✦ The 3 Most Important Things To Do This Month ✦
1

Nov 8 to 12: write your offer in plain words and let it sit. The New Moon on the 8th opens a fresh start with clients. Write one page that says who the offer is for, what they walk away with, and what it costs. Then leave it. Your clarity comes over a few nights, so the version you still like on the 12th is the one you use.

2

Nov 14 to 15: send the version that still felt right after a few nights. Your thinking and attraction energy both turned forward on the 13th. Send the offer to your warm people first: past buyers, past clients and your email list. The Vault Drop lands on the 15th too, so let the two share one strong week of emails and posts.

3

Nov 16 to 18: hold your prices and your limits. These three days put a spotlight on the part of your wiring that blurs your worth. You will feel the pull to discount, add bonuses or give sessions away. Decide your holiday and Black Friday plan before the 16th, after several nights of feeling the same way about it, and keep it as decided.
